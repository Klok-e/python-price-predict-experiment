# Replace Forward Proof with a Persistent Paper Account

Hard-cut the uninterrupted Forward Paper Proof runtime, Proof Clock, and pass/fail gate in favor of
a persistent, manually reset Paper Account operated by the local dashboard service. Historical
validation and holdout artifacts remain Development Evidence and may bootstrap the trader, but
paper-account results are exploratory and never claim real-world profitability; this supersedes the
Forward Paper Proof requirement in ADR-0003.
